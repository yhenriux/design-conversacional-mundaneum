## Resumo disponível

Generative artificial intelligence chatbots are powerful tools that support users with disabilities across diverse contexts, including work, communication, and everyday life.Specifically within the neurodivergent population, numerous studies have illustrated increased use of LLM chatbots in applications for mental health, well-being, education, and professional support, along with their further potential to serve this community.Our work builds on this scholarship by investigating how neurodivergent individuals use Large Language Model-based chatbots in real-life settings.We conducted interviews with 15 neurodivergent participants to understand their expansive use of these chatbots, which we present as use case categories.We then designed diary studies to capture prompting strategies participants employ within these use cases while conversing with the Large Language Model-based chatbot Gemini.Based on early analysis of our conversational dataset, we showcase specific prompting queries from participants, highlight participant strategies to refine Gemini's responses, and identify Gemini's strengths and weaknesses during interaction.
