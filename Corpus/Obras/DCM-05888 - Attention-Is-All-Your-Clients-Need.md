## Resumo disponível

Modern enterprises are witnessing a paradigm shift in customer interaction: from traditional text-based chatbots to video-first AI agents that deliver human-like, personalized experiences. In this paper, we argue that paying attention to clients-both in the technical sense (through advanced AI attention mechanisms) and the human sense (through face-to-face engagement)-is the key to client success and trust. We examine the limitations of conventional chatbots, present the advantages of embodied video agents (with a case focus on the Exthalpy platform), and detail the underlying technologies that enable these agents. Quantitative studies and industry data demonstrate that video-based AI assistants markedly improve customer satisfaction, trust, and business outcomes. We also outline the architectural elements (from Transformer-based language models to multimodal integration) that make such agents possible. The results suggest that "attention"-delivered via cutting-edge algorithms and human-centric design-truly is all your clients need for superior service.
