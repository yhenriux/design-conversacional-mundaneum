## Resumo disponível

Artificial intelligence (AI) and communication share a profound and evolving connection.From the early-stage research on chatbots designed to mimic human conversation and entertain users (Colby et al., 1971;Weizenbaum, 1966) to more recent studies on deepfakes where AI is used to create counterfeits of what a person says or does in audio or video (Carpenter, 2024;Wood et al., 2024), AI, as a powerful force, continues to evolve and transform communication in captivating ways for both academic and popular audiences.Throughout its evolution, AI has "emerged as both a catalyst and a protagonist" (p.ix).In this context, the book Revolutionizing Communication: The Role of Artificial Intelligence edited by Raquel V. Benítez Rojas and Francisco-Julián Martínez-Cano is an essential read.It offers timely insights into the rapidly advancing influence of AI on communication and provides retrospective, progressive, and prospective perspectives on the ways in which AI reshapes communication across various facets of the digital era.The book consists of 16 chapters that can be grouped into four sections: (a) the foundation of AI and communication products; (b) linguistic and interactive communication by AI; (c) AI in traditional communication industries; and (d) ethics and the potential future of AI in communication.Starting with the development of AI from its theoretical inception in the 1940s to its tangible advancements in the present day, Chapter 1, "Artificial Intelligence: Genesis, Development, and Future," highlights significant milestones, influential theorists, and pivotal moments that have gradually shaped the field, offering readers a comprehensive overview of AI's growth from abstract concepts to real-world applications.Chapters 2 and 3, entitled "The Evolution of Animation and Motion Graphics through Contemporary AI Technologies" and "A Look at the Crossroads of Artificial Intelligence, Films, Video Games, and Virtual Reality" respectively, discuss AI's significant influence on communication products, particularly the ways how AI has transformed animation, motion graphics, video games, films, and virtual reality (VR).
