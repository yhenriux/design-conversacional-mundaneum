## Resumo disponível

This work introduces the design and development of an intelligent chatbot capable of handling queries from both PDF documents and Excel spreadsheets using advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) methods.Conventional techniques for extracting information from lengthy text or complex tables are often slow, manual, and inefficient.The proposed chatbot addresses these issues by allowing users to interact through natural language queries, delivering quick and precise results.It applies NLP techniques such as tokenization, named entity recognition, and semantic analysis to interpret intent and identify relevant content from unstructured PDFs and structured Excel data.At its core, a transformer-based language model enhances query understanding and generates context-aware responses.The system provides a simple, user-friendly interface with cross-platform compatibility, making it accessible to diverse users.Evaluation shows significant improvements in response speed, accuracy, and user experience compared with traditional search approaches.Moreover, it supports scalability, contextual comprehension, and integration with additional data sources.These features make it highly suitable for academic research, business intelligence, and efficient data management.Overall, the chatbot delivers a unified and intelligent solution for seamless interaction with both structured and unstructured information.
