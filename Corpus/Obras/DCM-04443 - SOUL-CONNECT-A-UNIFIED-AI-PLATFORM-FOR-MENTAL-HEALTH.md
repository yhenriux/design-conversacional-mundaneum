## Resumo disponível

Soul Connect is a unified mental health chatbot designed to provide accessible and compassionate support to individuals experiencing mental health challenges. Mental health remains one of the most underaddressed areas in healthcare, with millions of people worldwide struggling to access timely and judgment-free support. Soul Connect bridges this gap by offering a conversational AI-powered platform where individuals dealing with any form of mental illness can openly communicate, express their concerns, and receive meaningful responses in a safe and supportive environment. The primary goal of Soul Connect is to make mental health support available to anyone, anytime, without the barriers of cost, stigma, or availability of professionals. The chatbot is designed to engage users in empathetic, context-aware conversations that help them feel heard and understood. By focusing on user well-being and emotional accessibility, Soul Connect aims to serve as a first point of contact for individuals seeking mental health guidance and emotional support. This paper presents the design, development, and evaluation of Soul Connect, highlighting its potential as a scalable solution to address the growing global mental health crisis.
