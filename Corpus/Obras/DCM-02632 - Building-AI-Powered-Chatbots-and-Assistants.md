## Resumo disponível

Chatbots are among the most visible and widely adopted applications of generative AI. Creating a chatbot or assistant that works well requires thoughtful design, reliable handling of edge cases, and an understanding of how humans communicate. This chapter discusses how to build an AI-powered chatbot. It explores best practices for conversation design, techniques for managing memory and context, and UX patterns for AI assistants. SimpleBot treats each message as a one-off interaction. System prompts are an essential tool for steering the assistant's behavior. They tell the model what its role is, what kinds of questions to answer, and what to avoid. For a single-product assistant like this, the system prompt can contain everything it needs to stay on track. Customer service chatbots have become indispensable tools for organizations aiming to improve efficiency and user experience.
