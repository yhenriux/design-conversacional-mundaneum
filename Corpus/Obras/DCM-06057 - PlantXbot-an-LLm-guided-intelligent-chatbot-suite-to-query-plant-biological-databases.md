## Resumo disponível

Biological discovery increasingly depends on large, curated genomic and multi-omics databases, yet effective access remains limited by complex schemas, rigid query interfaces, and the need for programming expertise. Large language models (LLMs) enable natural language interaction with complex systems; however, their application to structured biological databases is hindered by inaccuracies, limited reproducibility, and unreliable query translation. Here, we introduce PlantXBot ( http://www.nipgr.ac.in/PlantXBot ), a context-aware conversational framework that enables reliable natural language querying of biological databases by integrating deterministic NLP-to-SQL translation, semantic retrieval, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Its flexible architecture supports both direct querying of individual databases and coordinated searches across multiple domain-specific repositories through specialized conversational agents, translating user intent into structured queries and substantially reducing manual effort. Benchmarked on real-world plant genomics and peptide databases, PlantXBot achieves high intent-routing accuracy with minimal hallucination, demonstrating reliable intent-to-query translation and marked gains in the efficiency and reliability of biological data retrieval. PlantXBot thus provides an extensible, reproducible framework for LLM-mediated access to structured omics data, broadening accessibility for non-technical researchers and enabling intuitive discovery in plant genomics and bioinformatics.
