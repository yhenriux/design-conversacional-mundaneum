## Resumo disponível

The use of AI (Artificial Intelligence) in education has expanded the perspectives of pedagogical mediation on the conceptual framework of inclusive education, particularly in supporting students with ADHD (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder). Considering the cognitive demands associated with grounded attention, organization, and self-regulation of learning, this article analyzes the development and pedagogical application of a personalized educational chatbot, called TutorIA Foco+, designed for basic education students with ADHD, especially in middle and high school, in public and private institutions. This is an applied research, of a qualitative nature, with an exploratory and descriptive approach, which does not involve controlled experiments or statistical validation. The investigation is based on the analysis of the chatbot's pedagogical design, its interaction structure, and the personalization mechanisms incorporated, based on previously defined educational criteria such as communicational clarity, predictability of the interactive flow, cognitive organization, and stimulation of self-regulation of learning. The results of the qualitative analysis indicate that organizing content into microblocks, combined with structured interactions and objective language, can offer possibilities for supporting cognitive organization and monitoring the learning process in digital educational environments. It is concluded that the analyzed chatbot can act as a complementary resource for pedagogical mediation in the context of inclusive education, without replacing the role of the teacher, in accordance with principles of human-centeredness, ethics, transparency, and responsibility in the use of AI in education.
