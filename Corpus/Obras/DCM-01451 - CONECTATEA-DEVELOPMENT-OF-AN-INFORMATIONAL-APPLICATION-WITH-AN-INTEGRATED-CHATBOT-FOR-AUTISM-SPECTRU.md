## Resumo disponível

Considering the impact of digital misinformation on Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), access to scientifically validated knowledge is crucial. The objective is to develop ConectaTEA, a mobile application with an integrated chatbot for informational support and mapping of specialized services in Paragominas, Pará. To this end, a descriptive and exploratory research is conducted, including UML modeling, prototyping in Figma, and hybrid development with React Native, TypeScript, and the Gemini API, followed by usability testing with specialists. Thus, it is observed that the system achieved high navigation fluidity and 90% overall satisfaction due to its low-sensory-stimulation interface, despite minor responsiveness and history glitches. It is concluded that the union between assistive technologies and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) guidelines is strategic to democratize reliable information, promoting autonomy and social inclusion for the autistic community.
