## Resumo disponível

The Constitutional Court of Peru faces challenges in the management of helpdesk processes, which affect the efficiency and quality of the service provided to internal users. To solve this problem, it is proposed to implement a multiplatform system that provides a mixed service, i.e., support from a chatbot and intervention from specialized personnel. For the methodology, a survey was carried out specifically targeting the internal staff of the institution. In addition, an expert validation was carried out to present and evaluate the developed prototypes.
