## Resumo disponível

This paper presents the integration of Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and a ticket creation System in iBot, a chatbot designed to efficiently handle customer queries and complaints.The integration of OCR technology enables iBot to extract text from images, such as identity cards like aadhar cards and driving licenses, thereby automating the data entry process and reducing manual errors.On the other hand, the ticket creation system enables iBot to assign priority to the tickets based on their urgency and severity, thereby enabling faster resolution of issues.The paper describes the architecture of iBot and explains the various components of OCR and the ticket creation system.The OCR module uses deep learning algorithms to recognize text from images and converts it into a machine-readable format.The results show that iBot handled customer queries and complaints with high accuracy and efficiency, improving customer satisfaction and reducing the workload of customer support executives.
