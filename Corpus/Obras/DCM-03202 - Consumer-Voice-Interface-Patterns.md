## Resumo disponível

Voice User Interfaces (VUI) are a relatively new concept, although voice applications have been around for several years now. The invisible and transient character of sound led to believe that there was no user interface. This misconception rapidly disappeared with the technology advances that triggered the development of novel and rich voice applications for corporate use as well as in cars, phones and everyday appliances. The new and more complex functionality became a whole area known as Voice User Interfaces to address the di erent design forces and complexities behind a voice-based user interface. In the last years we began a pattern language for designing voice applications and documenting the aforementioned design tensions and common practices. In this paper, we continue with this work focusing on more complex issues such as authentication and consumer appliances interfaces.
