## Resumo disponível

This chapter explores the transformative role of web-based design and development in the digital age. It traces the evolution of web design, emphasizing user-centred principles, mobile responsiveness, and the e-commerce revolution. The chapter highlights emerging trends such as AI integration, Progressive Web Apps (PWAs), voice user interface (VUI), AR and VR, and dark mode design. It also underscores the importance of web accessibility. Ultimately, web design's continued evolution empowers individuals, businesses, and organizations in an ever-changing digital landscape.
