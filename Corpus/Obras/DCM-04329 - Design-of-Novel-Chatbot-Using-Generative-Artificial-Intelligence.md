## Resumo disponível

Chatbots have emerged as versatile tools for human-computer interaction, offering automated conversational experiences across diverse domains. The integration of Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) techniques has revolutionized chatbot design, enabling machines to generate human-like responses and adapt to complex user queries dynamically. This paper explores the design principles, methodologies, and innovations associated with developing chatbots using Generative AI. We review the underlying technologies, including deep learning architectures, natural language processing (NLP) techniques, and conversational modeling approaches. Additionally, we discuss the challenges and ethical considerations inherent in chatbot design, such as data privacy, bias mitigation, and user trust. Through case studies and practical examples, we highlight best practices and emerging trends in the field, emphasizing the potential of Generative AI-powered chatbots to enhance user engagement, streamline customer support processes, and facilitate personalized interactions across various domains.
