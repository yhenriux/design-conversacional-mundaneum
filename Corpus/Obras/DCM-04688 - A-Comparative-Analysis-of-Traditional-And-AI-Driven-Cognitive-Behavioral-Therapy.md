## Resumo disponível

Rapidly evolving AI technology is making inroads into various industries, including the field of mental health therapy, in the form of Chatbots designed for AI-driven cognitive behavioral therapy.This paper will explore the application of AI in traditional cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), focusing on the impacts, applications, accessibility, and efficacy of Chatbots compared to conventional CBT methods.Through a comprehensive literature review, this paper seeks to explore how AI chatbots can provide immediate and accessible emotional support to users.This paper will examine difficulties that may arise due to AIdriven CBT methods.These challenges include ethical issues, effectiveness, and the simplification of mental health processes.One AI-driven CBT this research paper focuses on is Chatbots.This paper concludes by outlining the most effective strategy for utilizing AI-driven methods to enhance the efficacy of AI chatbots in addressing mental health problems such as anxiety and depression.
