## Resumo disponível

Chatbots are increasingly being adopted to provide instant query resolution in various domains. This paper discusses the design, implementation, and evaluation of a chatbot application built using Flutter, specifically tailored to address agricultural queries. The chatbot employs a predefined question-answer (FAQ) model and state management through the provider package to deliver a lightweight yet robust user experience. This study also explores the challenges, outcomes, and future scope of such applications in the context of scalability, adaptability, and user engagement.
