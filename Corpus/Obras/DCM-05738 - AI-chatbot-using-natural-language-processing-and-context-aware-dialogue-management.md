## Resumo disponível

The development of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) has led to the creation of increasingly sophisticated conversational agents. Traditional chatbots are typically based on predefined dialogues or intent-matching rules, making it challenging to maintain context throughout multi-turn conversations. Recent advances in Large Language Models (LLMs) have shown promise in context-aware dialogues with human-like responses. This study presents the design and implementation of an AI chatbot that utilizes NLP techniques and context-aware dialogue management for engaging in natural conversations with users. The proposed system is built using the Flask framework and incorporates text preprocessing, intent detection, sentiment analysis, named entity recognition, dialogue state tracking, and the Hugging Face’s Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct language model for response generation. The chatbot is designed to store conversation logs in an SQLite database and employs dialogue summarization to maintain conversational context while reducing the prompt size during subsequent interactions. The experimental results demonstrate that our approach can effectively enhance the response relevance, conversation coherence, and scalability compared to traditional chatbot approaches. The design of the chatbot allows for easy integration of future innovations such as retrieval-augmented generation methods and transformer-based intent classification models.
