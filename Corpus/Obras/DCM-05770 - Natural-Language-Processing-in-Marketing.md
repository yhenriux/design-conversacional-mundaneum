## Resumo disponível

Natural language processing (NLP) is a technology that helps computers grasp, respond to, and process our language. NLP has a wide range of applications, including machine translation, speech recognition, sentiment analysis, information extraction, and text summarization, to name just a few. This involves teaching machines to understand the complexities of human language, including its semantics, syntax, and context, among other things. The chapter delves into the core techniques of text preprocessing and feature extraction, pivotal steps in transforming unstructured textual data into a structured format suitable for advanced analysis. It explores some of the key NLP techniques and how they can be leveraged to optimize marketing efforts. These techniques include sentiment analysis, topic modeling, named entity recognition, and text classification, each of which has a distinct role in transforming unstructured text data into actionable insight. The chapter also explores the evolution, best practices, and implications of chatbots and voice assistants in marketing.
