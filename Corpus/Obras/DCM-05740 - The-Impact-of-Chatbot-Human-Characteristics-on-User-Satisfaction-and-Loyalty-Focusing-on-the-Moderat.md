## Resumo disponível

In this study, we attempted to determine the impact of chatbots' human characteristics on users' satisfaction and loyalty, and to determine the moderating effect of chatbots' social presence by dividing them into groups with high perception and low perception groups. The survey was conducted online and a total of 300 responses were collected and analyzed using structural equation modeling to verify the hypothesis. Through empirical analysis, it was confirmed that the human characteristics of chatbots have a positive influence on user satisfaction and loyalty using chatbots. In addition, in order to confirm the moderating effect of social presence of the chatbot service, differences were confirmed by comparing groups with a high perception of social presence and groups with a low perception of social presence. The results of the comparative study showed that among the chatbot characteristics, the group with a high perception of social presence The path from functionality to satisfaction was rejected. It is believed that social presence played an important role for users using chatbot services. There were also differences in the path coefficients of the two groups. These research results provide a priori research results confirming the importance of social presence in companies that develop and use chatbots.
