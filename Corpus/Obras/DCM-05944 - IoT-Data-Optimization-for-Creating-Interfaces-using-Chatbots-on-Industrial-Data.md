## Resumo disponível

This research paper explores the integration of Industrial Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop an innovative AI chatbot tailored for the industrial sector. The chatbot aims to optimize resource allocation, minimize downtime through proactive predictive maintenance, enhance energy efficiency, and provide real-time data insights. By leveraging this technology, the primary objective is to drive a transformative shift in manufacturing processes, fostering greater efficiency, sustainability, and cost-effectiveness. The research underscores the transformative potential of the AI chatbot across critical domains such as resource management, predictive maintenance, energy optimization, and analytics, emphasizing its impact on industrial operations. By analyzing existing solutions and rationale behind the chosen approach, the study elucidates how our proposed chatbot solution enhances performance and addresses key imperatives for success. Through effective implementation this solution not only yields immediate benefits but also lays the groundwork for future advancements in the field.
