## Resumo disponível

Influencing electricity consumption using feedback tools such as Conversational Agentic systems (CAS) presents a novel, yet unexplored opportunity in influencing usage behavior in households.Agentic information systems with conversational agents (CA) capability could actively engage users in comparison to passive feedback endpoints to provide dynamic, outcome-based, role-aware personalized real-time feedback.CAs could deliver the salient information on energy use, facilitating engagement and technology-assisted behavior change.Existing feedback interventions largely focus on individuals, neglecting the complex social dimensions and collective practices of household decision-making.Our study proposes a dialogue-based CAS operationalized through smartphone application as the IS intervention to influence household energy consumption and promote pro-environmental behavior.The developed CAS will be evaluated with household dyads in fluid roles, with each dyad presented scenarios varying along two dimensions, namely activity type and intended outcome behavior, mediated by CAS at varying levels of delegated autonomy.
