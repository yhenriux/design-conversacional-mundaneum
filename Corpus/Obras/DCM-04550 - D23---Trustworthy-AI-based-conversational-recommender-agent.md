## Resumo disponível

Integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) into the GILL Hub platform introduces an advanced, ethical, and scalable AI-powered recommendation tool to foster gender equality. This chatbot, designed as a recommender system, simplifies user interaction by providing personalized and efficient navigation through the platform’s extensive resources spanning sectors such as education, healthcare, and employment. Its development emphasizes ethical principles, transparency, and inclusivity, ensuring alignment with the European Commission’s Trustworthy AI guidelines. The system architecture leverages three primary components: • OpenAI for advanced language models and embeddings, • Pinecone for managing a robust vector database, and • the AI Power WordPress plugin for dynamic content indexing and security. OpenAI’s models, including GPT-4 and embedding tools, process user queries and platform content to generate precise, contextually relevant recommendations. Pinecone enables the efficient retrieval of this information through vector similarity searches, ensuring real-time responsiveness and scalability. The AI Power plugin automates new content extraction, transformation, and indexing, maintaining an up-to-date vector database while ensuring data integrity and compliance with privacy standards such as General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Ethical adherence underpins the chatbot’s design. OpenAI prioritizes transparency, privacy, and fairness, employing mechanisms like reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) to refine model behaviour. Pinecone secures data with encryption and robust access controls while promoting transparency and minimizing data retention. AI Power enhances security through features such as IP anonymization and user authorization. In practice, the chatbot offers to users a seamless experience for accessing gender-related content by transforming natural language queries into actionable recommendations. For instance, when prompted for articles on gender biases in entrepreneurship, the chatbot identifies relevant resources and provides direct links to curated content on the GILL platform. This efficient retrieval process empowers stakeholders—policymakers, educators, and advocates alike—to make informed decisions and advance gender equality. By adhering to ethical guidelines, ensuring robust privacy protections, and maintaining transparent operations, the GILL chatbot establishes a trusted and effective tool for promoting gender innovation. Its integration reflects the strategic application of AI to reduce barriers to knowledge access, foster equity, and support a fairer, more inclusive society.
