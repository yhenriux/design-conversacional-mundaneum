## Resumo disponível

Numerous mainstream news organizations have adopted chatbots as an emerging channel for delivering personalized news services. However, the news presentation style of chatbots may not satisfy users’ personalization needs due to the “one-size-fits-all” design. In this context, we conducted two within-subject studies to investigate the impacts of news reader types, namely “dipper”, “tracker”, and “reviewer” on users’ preference for different interfaces for news selection and reading. Our preliminary findings reveal that all these types of news readers perceived a higher level of ease of selection when using the carousel design. For “trackers” and “reviewers”, the question-driven design resulted in a higher level of perceived ease of reading than the in-conversation design. Finally, I discuss my findings and provide insights into personalized user interface design for news chatbots.
