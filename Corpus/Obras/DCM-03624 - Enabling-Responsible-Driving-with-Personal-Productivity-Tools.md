## Resumo disponível

A large number of drivers are using devices such as mobile phones PDAs, pagers and computers to make themselves more productive while driving their cars. These devices, however, can be distracting and therefore endanger the driver and others. The authors of this paper examine the use of these products along with new human interface technologies that utilize speech as both input and output. They include a case study using the Mobile-Aria solution. In this paper they suggest that the most important elements of an in-vehicle system that would enable drivers to watch the road and keep their hands on the wheel while using a telematics system would need to incorporate the following: an optional display and graphical user interface, a barge button to activate speech recognition, comprehensible text-to- speech system, a well-designed voice user interface, and highly accurate speech recognition.
