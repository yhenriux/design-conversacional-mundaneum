## Resumo disponível

Chatbots are everywhere and no wonder why. In business context, chatbots have gained recognition in being helpful tools in customer support, offering businesses the opportunity to provide better customer experiences in a cost conscious manner. Despite the recent growth in interest, the academic literature regarding chatbots in e-commerce from a customer’s perspective isn’t complete as existing literature lacks studies about chatbots as a part of online customer experience. To study Finnish consumers’ perceptions and attitudes towards chatbots in e-commerce retail and chatbots’ impact on online customer experience, a qualitative study was conducted. To answer the set research question semi-sturctured interviews were held and interviewee’s interaction with IKEA’s chatbot Aatos was observed. The findings of this study show, that Finnish consumers’ perceptions and attitudes towards chatbots in e-commerce retail have an opposing narrative when compared to how businesses view chatbots. Even though chatbots are developed customers’ intentions in mind, the result doesn’t seem to live up to the intentions. Negative perceptions towards chatbots seems to evolve from negative past experiences with chatbots, often relating to customer being misunderstoond and thus receiving wrong and unclear asnwers. To live up to the customer centric intentions around chatbots, businesses should focus on the fundamental purpose behind chatbots, being the job of providing meaningful information in a humanlike manner. It is also recommended, that chatbots’ accessibility should be highlighted over availability by having the entire online customer journey in mind when designing chatbots. Chatbot’s carry loads of future potential and thus Artificial Intelligence technologies should be invested in and chatbot personalization should be carried out by having the privacy concerns in mind. This master’s thesis contributes to the existing literature on chatbots and online customer experience and fills the gap in literature about consumer’s motives behind chatbot usage and chatbots’ impact on online customer experience.
