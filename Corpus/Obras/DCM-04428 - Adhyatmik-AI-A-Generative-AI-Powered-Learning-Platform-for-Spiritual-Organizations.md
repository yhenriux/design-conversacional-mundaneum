## Resumo disponível

This paper presents Adhyatmik AI, a GenAI Chatbot designed to make spiritual education scalable and personalized. Initial pilot development for the Avadhoot Sampradaya propagated by Shri Pantmaharaj, Balekundri and scalable to the wider Datta Sampradaya. This platform fixes the problems with other repositories that give out spiritual information in pieces and in a general way. It uses fine-tuned large language models, natural language processing, sentiment analysis, and multimodal GenAI techniques to give users accurate, interactive, and context-aware experiences with text, audio, and video content. Some of its most important features are adapting content to fit the model persona and feelings of each user at the stage of their spiritual journey (for example, beginner, disciple, or mentor) and putting spiritual values into the AI to make sure that interactions are kind and moral. It uses social media platforms to help mentors and disciples work together. The goal is to preserve doctrinal integrity aligned with the principles of responsible AI and enable structured mentorship while using technology to help seekers progress effectively on their Adhyatmik path at a faster pace, which is the need of the hour.
