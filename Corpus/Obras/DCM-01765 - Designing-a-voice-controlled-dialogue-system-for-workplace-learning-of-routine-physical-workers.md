## Resumo disponível

The design of conversational user interfaces (CUIs) remains challenging because CUIs do not follow the conventional ways we conduct graphical user interface (GUI) designs. Furthermore, conversational experience design is ongoing research in the field of HCI. However, research is still scarce on voice-controlled dialogue systems design in which people can interact with unique audio resources such as podcasts and textual materials such as Wikis by auditory and vocal channels for learning in workplace contexts. We report the preliminary results obtained from Wizard of Oz tests conducted with two participants to find effective and valuable ways to create dialogue scripts for our proposed task-oriented modularised voice-controlled dialogue system for workplace learning of routine physical workers. The results are very insightful for our next design steps of the proposed system.
