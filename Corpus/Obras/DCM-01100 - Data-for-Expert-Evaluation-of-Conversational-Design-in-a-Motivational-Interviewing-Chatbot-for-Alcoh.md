## Resumo disponível

This dataset contains anonymized expert-rating data supporting the findings reported in the manuscript “Expert Evaluation of Conversational Design in a Motivational Interviewing Chatbot for Alcohol Risk Screening.” The dataset includes ratings of clarity, coherence, relevance, and congruence provided by eight expert judges divided into two expert groups. Direct identifiers and original initials were removed. For selected items, congruence was not applicable by design; these values are represented as blank cells and are documented in the accompanying README file.
