## Resumo disponível

This volume brings together the papers presented at the 3rd Workshop on Multimodal Output Generation (MOG 2010). Charton et al. investigate the problem of transition from a textual content to a graphical representation. Kriegel et al.contribute with a paper on multimodal interaction in a collaborative role-play game. Lohmann et al. propose the use of tactile maps as a means to communicate spatial knowledge for visually impaired people. O’Neill et al. present a framework for the development of spoken and multimodal dialogue systems based on a dialogue act hierarchy. Finally, van Welbergen et al. discuss action planning for the generation of speech and gestures for virtual agents.In addition, the proceedings include abstracts provided by our invited speakers.
