## Resumo disponível

Conversational User Interfaces (CUIs) are becoming central touchpoints in everyday health information seeking, self-management, and service access. Yet in digital public health, they are often narrowly framed as tools for question-answering or triage. This workshop asks what becomes possible when CUIs are treated not only as instruments but also as socio-technical actors that can shape public health communication, participation, trust, and care pathways. We invite researchers and practitioners from HCI, public health, design, and AI to examine opportunities and tensions around conversational systems in prevention, health literacy, behavior change, and equitable access to services. Particular attention will be given to responsible and human-centered CUI research targeted to digital public health. Through presentations and collaborative discussion, the workshop aims to build an interdisciplinary agenda and strategies for designing CUIs that meet the contemporary needs of diverse patients in an increasingly digitalized world.
