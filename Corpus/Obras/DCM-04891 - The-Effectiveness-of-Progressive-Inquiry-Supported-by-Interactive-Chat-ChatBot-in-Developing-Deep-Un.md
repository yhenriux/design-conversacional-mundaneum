## Resumo disponível

This sudy investigated the effect of the Progressive Inquiry Model (PIM) supported by interactive chatbots on developing deep understanding among middle school students in Makkah Al-Mukarramah. A quasi-experimental design was used with two groups (experimental and control), comprising 64 students equally distributed. The experimental group was taught using PIM supported by chatbots, while the control group followed the traditional method. A deep understanding test was administered pre- and post-intervention.The results showed statistically significant differences (α ≤ 0.05) in favor of the experimental group in the post-test. A high effect size was found (ω² = 0.64), and the Blake Modified Gain Ratio reached 3.45, indicating substantial learning gains.
