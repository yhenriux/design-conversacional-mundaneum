## Resumo disponível

In this paper, we describe the process of developing an AI chatbot that serves as a coach to help individuals to set and achieve personal goals.The chatbot was developed using over 50 transcripts from coaching conversations between human coaches and individuals in the Examen Tu Salud health and wellness program created by our team.Over four sessions, the AI Chatbot uses a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system to examine the user message and find similar responses from the coaching transcripts.The top three responses are then evaluated based on the context, and a coaching response is generated and displayed to the participant.Based on early evaluation of the chatbot prototype, it generates appropriate, contextuallyrelevant responses to the participant.We plan to conduct a detailed evaluation of the app in future work.
