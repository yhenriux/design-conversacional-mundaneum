## Resumo disponível

The integration of technology into healthcare has paved the way for innovative solutions like medical chatbots, designed to streamline patient care and support. This project presents the development of a medical chatbot leveraging the Django framework, aiming to provide accurate, efficient, and user friendly medical assistance.
