## Resumo disponível

This presentation examines the role of technical communicators in shaping ethical and equitable implementations of generative AI (genAI) technologies with a focus on intercultural communication and localized knowledge-making. Through three use cases, the authors demonstrate how technical communicators can address algorithmic biases and promote inclusive practices by exploring how women activists in South Asia develop algorithmic literacies for digital advocacy despite gender and geographical digital divides; analyzing university chatbots' limitations in serving international students, revealing issues with machine learning bias, accessibility, and linguistic barriers; and theorizing an AI-powered chatbot for a Zine Lab focused on gender justice. The authors argue that understanding specific local contexts and cultures is essential when implementing genAI technologies, especially for marginalized communities, and that technical communicators can play a crucial role in developing more equitable technological futures.
