## Resumo disponível

The field of Artificial Intelligence (AI) is in a constant state of evolution, with conversational agents gaining prominence across diverse domains, including food product analysis. This evolution is crucial, especially considering the rise in foods with suboptimal nutritional value, which calls for proactive measures like food reformulation. Food reformulation involves modifying the composition of food products to improve their nutritional profile while maintaining taste and sensory attributes, making it a key strategy for promoting a healthier food supply. Focusing on this problem, this work was developed at OmniumAI, a spin-off from the University of Minho specialized in AI and data science solutions for the life sciences, making it uniquely positioned to develop advancements in the intersection of AI and food science. More specifically, this work aims to develop a copilot chatbot for the CibusAI platform. Developed by OmniumAI, this platform serves as a tool for food companies, facilitating the identification of alternative ingredients that possess similar functional and organoleptic characteristics. This platform integrates AI-driven food reformulation techniques to optimize flavor and nutritional attributes, providing a sophisticated solution for the industry. The copilot chatbot, a crucial addition to CibusAI, is designed to enhance the user experience by offering robust support in navigating the extensive information within the platform. The development of the proposed chatbot departs from conventional parametric fine-tuning techniques by integrating external chemistry and food-related tools, significantly expanding its knowledge-base in the fields of chemistry and food science. The chatbot interacts with PubChem and RDKit to retrieve chemical and nutritional data, while also using Machine Learning (ML) models to predict compound attributes such as taste, odor, and antioxidant activity. Additionally, it connects to vector databases hosted on Databricks, which store regulatory information about food products, forming a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) solution. This hybrid approach not only reflects the advancements in conversational agents but also highlights OmniumAI’s commitment to staying at the forefront of developments in both AI and food science.
