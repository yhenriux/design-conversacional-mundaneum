## Resumo disponível

The quality of a website's design greatly impacts its effectiveness, and designers must stay updated on rapidly changing trends to remain competitive. Key trends in web design for 2024 include minimalism, dark mode, and animation, which enhance user experience and visual appeal. Emerging technologies like augmented reality and voice user interfaces are transforming web development, making digital interactions more engaging and efficient.
