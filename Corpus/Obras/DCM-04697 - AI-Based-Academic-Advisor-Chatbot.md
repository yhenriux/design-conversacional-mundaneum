## Resumo disponível

In today’s rapidly evolving higher education landscape, universities are challenged with providing timely, personalized academic advising to a growing and diverse student population. Traditional advising models—largely dependent on in-person appointments, manual degree audits, and limited office hours—often fall short in meeting the expectations of digital-native learners who seek instant, mobile-first interactions. To bridge this gap, this project introduces ASIA (Academic Support Intelligence Assistant), an AI-based academic advising chatbot designed to supplement and enhance traditional advising services by delivering intelligent, accessible, and scalable support. ASIA leverages advanced technologies, including Natural Language Processing (NLP), predictive analytics, and Management Information Systems (MIS), to simulate human-like advising conversations and provide real-time responses to common student queries. Seamlessly integrated with institutional Student Information Systems (SIS), the chatbot is capable of retrieving up-to-date academic data such as GPA, course history, registration status, and graduation requirements. This integration enables ASIA to assist students with tasks such as course selection, degree planning, deadline tracking, and policy clarification, while also offering proactive nudges and personalized recommendations based on past academic performance. Beyond academic planning, ASIA functions as a digital bridge to other university departments by directing students to financial aid resources, wellness services, tutoring options, and more. The chatbot’s design emphasizes inclusivity and accessibility, offering features such as multilingual support and simple user interfaces to accommodate students from varied backgrounds and technical proficiencies (Essel et al., 2022). Importantly, ASIA also enhances the role of human advisors by reducing their workload on repetitive tasks, allowing them to focus on high-impact mentorship and complex academic guidance. This project follows a user-centered, iterative design methodology, incorporating feedback from students, academic advisors, and IT staff to ensure usability, relevance, and institutional alignment. Key metrics such as system accuracy, student engagement, and advising efficiency will be evaluated to assess the chatbot’s impact. Ethical considerations around data privacy, FERPA compliance, transparency, and equity are central to the system’s development and deployment. Ultimately, ASIA aspires to redefine the academic advising experience by combining the speed and scalability of AI with the empathy and insight of human advisors. The chatbot represents a forward-thinking response to institutional goals around retention, graduation rates, and student satisfaction. As the system evolves, future enhancements will include machine learning capabilities to identify at-risk students, deeper integration with learning and financial platforms, and expanded support for career planning. ASIA stands as a model for how artificial intelligence can be ethically and effectively deployed to support student success in modern higher education.
