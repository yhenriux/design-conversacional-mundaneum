## Resumo disponível

A QAS is a conversational agent designed to respond user queries. Natural Language Processing (NLP) enables to understand the user&s;s intent hidden within natural language questions. Traditional machine learning methods were limited by the amount of data used for training, restricting their capabilities. Deep Learning (DL) techniques have emerged as the most promising solution for QAS handling massive datasets. While significant progress has been made in NLP and the rise of DL, QAS still face limitations. They struggle with subtle nuances, complex inquiries, and adapting to unforeseen situations. These limitations hinder their real-world application. In this regard, the aim is to analyze the current state of DL-based QAS to address key queries. Hence, a case study on DL-based chatbot system is performed to answer user queries on breast cancer dataset. The evaluation results show the model achieves high accuracy (0.9747), with precision (0.9827), and recall (0.9504). Additionally, the low training loss (0.1121) suggests the model learned effectively from the training data. This chatbot system has the potential to improve access to accurate and reliable breast cancer information for patients, caregivers, and healthcare professionals.
