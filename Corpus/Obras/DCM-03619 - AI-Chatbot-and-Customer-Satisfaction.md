## Resumo disponível

Chatbot performance is considered one of the key elements influencing customer satisfaction in the online customer service environment. Nonetheless, despite chatbots being more common, limited studies have examined the connection between chatbot performance and customer satisfaction from users’ viewpoints. Therefore, the study focuses on the role of an AI chatbot in customer satisfaction. Considering interpretivist research philosophy, an inductive approach and qualitative method were employed in the study along with an open-ended questionnaire. Thematic analysis was used to analyze themes and patterns of the collected data. The study observes that AI chatbot interactions with customers are measured by the quality of response, their relevance, and the ability to resolve customer issues. It is also shown that response time, user-friendliness, usability, accessibility, and chatbot structure are a few factors related to the use of AI chatbots. Moreover, the performance of AI chatbots is assessed by their ability to provide instant assistance and efficiently handle routine matters. However, there is a limitation: users show dissatisfaction when the chatbot does not understand their questions or provides irrelevant answers. Theoretically, the study aligns with expectation confirmation theory (ECT), as customer satisfaction was strongly influenced by chatbot responses that met users’ expectations for accuracy, relevance, and convenience. The research indicates that companies need to boost the accuracy of their chatbots and provide better conversational experiences that are seamlessly integrated into human service systems. The findings also emphasize the importance of maintaining a balance between human support and automated services.
