## Resumo disponível

This repository contains the anonymized datasets collected during the evaluation of AstridBot, a chatbot designed to support novice users in performing Nmap network scanning tasks. The repository includes: (1) participants' responses to the Informed Consent Form (ICF), including demographic information and background experience used for participant characterization and selection, and (2) responses to the NASA Task Load Index (NASA-TLX) and System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaires, which were used to assess perceived cognitive workload and system usability after task completion. The datasets support the reproducibility of the experimental evaluation by providing the anonymized data used in the analysis. All personally identifiable information has been removed or anonymized to ensure participant privacy and comply with ethical research standards.
