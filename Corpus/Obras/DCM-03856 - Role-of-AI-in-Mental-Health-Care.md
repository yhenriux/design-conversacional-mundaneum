## Resumo disponível

Mental health encompasses emotional, psychological, and social well-being, influencing thoughts, emotions, and behaviors. Crucial for overall resilience, mental health issues can manifest in conditions requiring attention and support. Addressing these issues is vital to alleviating individual suffering and contributing to community well-being. Prioritizing mental health fosters compassion reduces stigma and builds resilient, supportive environments. Mental health care involves services for those facing challenges, spanning prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and recovery support. The integration of new technologies in mental health care has profoundly reshaped the identification of mental health disorders, bringing both promise and challenges. Artificial Intelligence enhances healthcare by analyzing data and images for diagnosis, improving operational efficiency, and personalizing medicine. In mental health, AI aids early detection, assesses conditions through data analysis, and offers personalized interventions via chatbots and virtual assistants. This extends mental health service accessibility, demonstrating AI's significant role in holistic well-being. This survey paper aims to gather valuable perspectives on mental health care and data on the applications, benefits, and challenges of AI in healthcare for improved patient care and outcomes.
