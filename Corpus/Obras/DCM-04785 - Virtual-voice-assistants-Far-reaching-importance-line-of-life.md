## Resumo disponível

The present research article is focused on a review of different aspects pertaining to virtual voice assistant system.Article looks into the history and capabilities of voice assistants in different applications, which have made users life easier.Such a system allows users to do searches and get information utilizing speech synthesis using a user-inputted voice command.Construction, government, security, healthcare, and finance are among the areas where voice assistants are making tremendous progress.Context-based comprehension has been a quantum leap forward in voice assistance technology in recent years, and it is now a critical component in people's life.
