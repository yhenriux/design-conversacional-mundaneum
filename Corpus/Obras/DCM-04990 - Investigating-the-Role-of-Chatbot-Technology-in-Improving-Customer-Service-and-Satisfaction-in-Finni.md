## Resumo disponível

Finland has seen the rapid expansion of the e-commerce sector and by 2013 the number of Finnish citizens aged 16 to 74 years who had used online shopping services reached more than 70 percent. As digital shopping services become more widespread, Finns are accustomed to interacting with chatbots at the level of online stores. This thesis explores the influence of chatbot technology on customer service and satisfaction in the context of Finnish online retailers, examining three main research questions, to understand what Finns consider the quality of chatbot service, what benefits may be provided by chatbot technology, and what improvements could be implemented to improve customer service. A qualitative method was used and the data was collected via interviews with 10 Finnish online shoppers with experience in customer service provided by chatbots. The interviews were analyzed by examining similarities across the themes raised by the interviewees, and by describing how these themes are related. Diverse results indicated that while many consumers find chatbots valuable for round-the-clock availability and sorting out basic issues quickly, many respondents showed clear levels of dissatisfaction when chatbots are not capable of understanding natural language or providing appropriate information, or do not give easy access to human service. Moreover, a contrast emerged between consumer expectations and actual delivery by the chatbot, and it was perceived as the key barrier to success. According to the results, the research proposes that Finnish e-commerce merchants should adopt AI chatbots with natural language processing technology and have escalation paths available for customers. Moreover, it encourages chatbot transparency and feedback mechanisms to facilitate continuous improvement in the service. Finally, this thesis introduces a Finnish perspective on chatbots from a consumer standpoint to the growing literature on chatbots within the realm of digital commerce. Keywords: Chatbot technology, Customer service, satisfaction
