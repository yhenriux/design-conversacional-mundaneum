## Resumo disponível

The Surabaya City Library’s information services are currently disorganized and require visitors to wait for administrative responses during operational hours. The book search and recommendation system only supports exact title matches, leading to a suboptimal user experience. To improve this, we propose a chatbot designed to provide general information, historical insights, holiday schedules, and book recommendations. The application uses the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) method, incorporating multi-collection data storage and a hybrid retriever that combines Cross-Encoder and Bi-Encoder techniques for better accuracy. Our research tested several retriever approaches, with the Hybrid Rerank method (40% Bi-Encoder and 60% Cross-Encoder) demonstrating superior performance. This method achieved Context Precision of 0.877 and Context Recall of 0.833. Out of 100 questions, only seven went unanswered, showing a high success rate. Additionally, it reduced token usage by up to 45% compared to the pure Bi-Encoder method.
