## Resumo disponível

This article investigates how technological and social innovations can be integrated into an inclusive design process to enhance accessibility and social inclusion in sports education for visually impaired children. Drawing on a review of the literature and case study analyses, the paper highlights innovative approaches such as movement-based games, smart playful objects, virtual and mixed reality applications, and voice user interfaces tailored to visually impaired users. It emphasizes the importance of co-design processes, inclusive sports policies, and teacher training as key enablers of inclusive practices. The identified benefits go beyond physical development, encompassing social interaction, emotional well-being, and educational growth. Nevertheless, the article also addresses persistent challenges, including the lack of standardized guidelines, material and social barriers, and accessibility issues. Finally, it offers recommendations focused on leveraging emerging technologies, fostering community participation, advancing policy advocacy, and enhancing teacher support, with the goal of building truly inclusive, equitable, and sustainable sports education environments for all children.
