## Resumo disponível

This project presents an AI-powered healthcare chatbot designed to assist users in the preliminary diagnosis of health conditions and provide basic medical advice through both text and voice interactions. Aimed at improving accessibility and empowering users, the chatbot utilizes natural language processing and symptom-checking algorithms to interpret user input, categorize symptoms as either minor or major, and deliver appropriate responses. For minor conditions, the chatbot suggests home remedies, dietary advice, and wellness tips. In cases of more serious symptoms, it advises users to consult a physician and provides initial recommendations, such as taking over-the-counter analgesics. A core feature of the system is its dynamic questioning approach, which personalizes health guidance based on users' responses, helping them understand their symptoms more effectively. By grouping symptoms and identifying potential patterns, the chatbot encourages early awareness and preventive action. This intelligent, interactive system supports individuals in managing their health proactively and seeks to reduce unnecessary medical visits, all while ensuring critical cases are escalated responsibly. The integration of voice and text-based communication ensures a user-friendly experience for diverse populations, including those with visual impairments or low literacy levels.
