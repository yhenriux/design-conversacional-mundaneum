## Resumo disponível

The Cognitive Behavioral AI Therapist (CBT-AI) is a clever conversational agent designed to assist users experiencing anxiety, stress, and negative thought patterns in their everyday lives. It identifies user emotions and cognitive distortions with context-aware natural language processing (NLP) and deep emotion reasoning and then applies Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) principles to stimulate supportive, educational and psychologically informed responses. The system uses transformer-based emotion classifiers and a cognitive-distortion detector and another component uses controllable response-generation models such as ‘DialoGPT’ or ‘GPT‑2’ to personalize responses interventions that may take the form of contributions to flow the conversation secure backend with MySQL mood logs and the conversation history enables longterm tracking of emotional trends and thought patterns, allowing the Artificial Intelligence (AI) to give continuity over sessions while preserving user privacy. The overall transformer-based emotion classifier achieved a macro F1 score of 0.82. The cognitive distortion analysis by the proposed model describes the overall accuracy of 78% and macro-F1 score of 0.75. Where Context-Aware response generation also increased the coherence and relevance of speech by 15 to 20%.
