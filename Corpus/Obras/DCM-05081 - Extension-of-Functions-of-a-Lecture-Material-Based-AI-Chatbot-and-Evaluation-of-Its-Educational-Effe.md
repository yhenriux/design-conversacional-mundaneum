## Resumo disponível

This paper proposes an AI chatbot designed to enhance lecture comprehension by integrating lecture materials with conversational context. Using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) based on lecture slides and video transcripts, the system also incorporates conversation history and an improved user interface to enhance continuity and usability. Deployed in three university courses—Software Engineering, Introduction to Information Network Engineering, and Experiments in Information Systems—the system’s effectiveness was evaluated through student questionnaires and free-response feedback. Results show that 65–77.5% of students found the system “quite helpful” or “very helpful,” with particularly high ratings in the Experiments in Information Systems course, indicating its effectiveness in supporting practical learning tasks. Qualitative analysis revealed that the chatbot aids in summarizing and explaining lecture content, enhancing students’ understanding and reflective learning. Unlike traditional chatbots or real-time Q&A tools like Zoom chat, the system offers personalized, context-aware support that draws on both lecture content and conversational history. While some limitations in response specificity and reliability remain, the study demonstrates the potential of this approach for improving learning support in university education.
