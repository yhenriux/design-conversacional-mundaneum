## Resumo disponível

Artificial intelligence (AI) has emerged as a transformative force in modern healthcare, revolutionizing how services are delivered, accessed and personalized. Among its most promising applications are AI-enabled chatbots and virtual assistants – intelligent conversational agents designed to simulate human interaction and support both patients and healthcare providers. This chapter analyses the growing integration of technologies within oncology and physiotherapy, two domains that demand personalized, continuous and multidisciplinary care. It reviews state-of-the-art technologies and evidence supporting their use, including natural language processing, machine learning and voice recognition systems. The chapter examines the benefits, such as scalability, cost-effectiveness and improved patient engagement, while also addressing pressing challenges, including ethical dilemmas, data privacy concerns, algorithmic biases and limitations in user trust and digital literacy. It outlines future directions and emerging trends such as integration with electronic health records, support for multiple languages and adaptive learning for personalized care.
