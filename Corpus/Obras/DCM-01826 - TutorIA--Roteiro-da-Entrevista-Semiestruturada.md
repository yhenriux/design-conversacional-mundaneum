## Resumo disponível

This document presents the semi-structured interview script used in the evaluation of the TutorIA system. TutorIA is a multimodal conversational agent designed to support the learning of introductory programming concepts through interaction with a virtual tutor in a 3D environment.The interview script was organized into thematic sections addressing general impressions of the system, perceptions of the interface and avatar, clarity of communication, and perceived pedagogical usefulness. The questions were designed to allow participants to freely describe their experiences and provide suggestions for improvement.This material is provided as supplementary research documentation to support transparency and reproducibility of the qualitative evaluation described in the associated study.
