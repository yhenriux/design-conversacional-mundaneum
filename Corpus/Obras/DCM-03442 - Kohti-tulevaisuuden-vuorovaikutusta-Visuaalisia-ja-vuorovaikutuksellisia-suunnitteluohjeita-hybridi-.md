## Resumo disponível

Recent advances in generative artificial intelligence have transformed conversational user interfaces from deterministic dialogue systems into adaptive, co-creative tools. This thesis examines how hybrid graphical–conversational user interfaces combine the complementary strengths of graphical and conversational interaction, and how generative AI introduces new interaction characteristics such as non-determinism, iterativeness, ambiguity, and increased demands for transparency and trust. Based on a literature review of academic HCI research and industry design guidelines, the thesis synthesizes a set of visual and interaction design patterns for generative conversational user interfaces. These patterns aim to support intent specification, iterative refinement, system state visibility, and user control, providing practical guidance for designing effective and trustworthy generative AI interfaces.
