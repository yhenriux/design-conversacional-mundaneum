## Resumo disponível

This study investigates whether a specific design choice in AI-based workplace support, the conversational style with which an AI opens its response to a stressed employee, affects three downstream outcomes: trust in the AI, psychological safety / disclosure comfort, and willingness to engage with the AI in future.
