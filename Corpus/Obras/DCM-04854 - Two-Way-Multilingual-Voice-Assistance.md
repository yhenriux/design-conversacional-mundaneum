## Resumo disponível

The multilingual voice assistant is an advanced platform that allows a user to communicate and interact in multiple languages. It realizes very accurate and seamless translation and transcription services through natural language processing (NLP) algorithms that provide the functionality of statistical machine translation (SMT) and neural machine translation (NMT) for accurate and fluent translation, transcription services, and multilingual language modeling to understand voice commands and support easy language switching.
