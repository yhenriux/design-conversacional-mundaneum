## Resumo disponível

The advent of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has revolutionized various sectors, and agriculture is no exception.With the ever-growing population and the increasing demand for food, there is a pressing need to enhance agricultural productivity while minimizing resource consumption and environmental impact.In this context, the development of an AI-driven chatbot tailored for cultivation purposes presents a promising solution.The proposed project aims to design and implement an AI-driven chatbot that offers real-time assistance to farmers and cultivators.The chatbot will utilize advanced AI techniques such as natural language processing (NLP), machine learning (ML), and computer vision to provide personalized recommendations, insights, and guidance throughout the cultivation process.The chatbot will integrate with sensors and IoT devices to monitor various parameters such as soil moisture levels, temperature, humidity, and crop health in real-time.The AI-driven chatbot for cultivation aims to empower farmers with actionable insights, personalized recommendations, and expert guidance, thereby enhancing agricultural productivity, sustainability, and profitability.By leveraging cutting-edge AI technologies, this project has the potential to revolutionize the way farming and cultivation are conducted, leading to a more efficient, resilient, and sustainable agricultural ecosystem.
