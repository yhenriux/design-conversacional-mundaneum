## Resumo disponível

The rapid integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in customer service has revolutionized how businesses interact with their customers. Among AI technologies, chatbots have emerged as a pivotal tool for enhancing customer experience, and offering real-time, personalized support. This research article explores the impact of AI-powered chatbots on brand loyalty and conversion rates. By analyzing case studies, existing literature, and empirical data, this study examines how chatbots influence customer satisfaction, trust, and long-term engagement, which are essential drivers of brand loyalty. Additionally, the article investigates the role of chatbots in guiding customers through the sales funnel, improving conversion rates by providing timely information, resolving queries, and enabling seamless interactions. Despite the numerous benefits, challenges such as technical limitations, privacy concerns, and the difficulty of measuring long-term effects remain. The findings of this study offer valuable insights for businesses aiming to leverage chatbot technology to strengthen brand loyalty and boost conversion rates.
