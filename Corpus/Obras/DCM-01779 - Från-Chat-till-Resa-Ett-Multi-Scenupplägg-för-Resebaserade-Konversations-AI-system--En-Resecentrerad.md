## Resumo disponível

Task-oriented dialogue systems have traditionally relied on modular pipelines that suffer from error propagation and limited adaptability. While large language models (LLMs) offer unprecedented capabilities in natural language understanding and generation, their application to goal-oriented conversations remains challenging due to issues in reliability, controllability, and adherence to structured workflows. This thesis presents a case study exploring Journey- Oriented Dialogue (JOD), an approach that extends traditional task-oriented dialogue by treating conversations as structured sequences of scenes that collectively work toward broader user goals of the whole customer journey. We propose a framework centered around Journey Instruction Schema-a structured, natural language blueprint that defines conversational flows through interconnected scenes. To evaluate this framework, we implement two architectural variants: a baseline single-stage architecture and a two-stage architecture that separates guidance and generation responsibilities. The later enables component specialization, including fine-tuning small models for the guidance stage while leveraging powerful proprietary models for generation. To evaluate our approach, we curate a novel dataset specifically designed for multi-scenario, multi-turn journey-oriented dialogues, which to our knowledge is the first annotated dataset incorporating journey-based conversation descriptions. Experiments show that the schema-driven approach achieves strong performance: the baseline architecture exceeds 88% scene accuracy, while the two-stage system consistently outperforms it across all metrics. Notably, finetuning a 3B parameter model for guidance achieves scene accuracy comparable to baseline GPT-4.1, demonstrating the practicality of cost-efficient specialized models. The dataset is available, providing an initial foundation for future research in journey-centric conversational AI systems, with real-world user validation remaining a critical next step.
