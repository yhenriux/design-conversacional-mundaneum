## Resumo disponível

The rising prevalence of mental health challenges, particularly among women, underscores the urgent need for accessible, empathetic, and gendersensitive support tools. This paper presents the development of a mental health support chatbot designed with a focus on the unique needs, preferences, and communication styles of women. Through interdisciplinary collaboration involving mental health professionals, AI developers, and gender studies experts, the chatbot was crafted to provide a safe, nonjudgmental, and culturally sensitive conversational experience. The system integrates natural language processing, sentiment analysis, and contextual understanding to deliver personalized support, self-care guidance, and resource referrals. User feedback and iterative testing with a diverse group of women informed the bot's tone, response patterns, and functionality. Preliminary evaluations indicate increased user satisfaction, trust, and willingness to engage compared to generic mental health bots. This paper discusses the design methodology, ethical considerations, and future directions for deploying gender-informed AI in mental health care.
