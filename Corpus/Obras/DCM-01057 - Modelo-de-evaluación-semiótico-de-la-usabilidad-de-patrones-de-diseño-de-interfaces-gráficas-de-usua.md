## Resumo disponível

<jats:p>The increase in the creation of mobile applications, called apps, where we find dozens of them for one topic, encourages users to discriminate against them, firstly because of their graphical interface and later because of their functionality. Therefore, this implies a change in the way apps are designed. App creators must offer higher quality in the graphical user interface (GUI-Graphical User Interface) and the app's functionalities. Quality includes aspects from the user's perspective, such as: ease of interaction with the GUI, intuitive workflow (navigation), correspondence between the response of the system and the functionality represented by an icon, a text, or a button, easy to learn, among others. These aspects must be studied to be improved and applied in the design of the apps. Under this premise, the problem of designing a MEtriSIG evaluation model was raised (Evaluation Model of the Signic Triad), which aims to be a tool to improve the development and performance of mobile applications, graphic interfaces, and the creation of patterns. Of graphic interface design since it has indicators that refer to signs that make up the interface and improve usability when applied correctly.</jats:p>
