## Resumo disponível

This paper analyzes the automation of customer relationships in e-commerce using chatbots, highlighting the conceptual, functional and economic differences between rulebased solutions and hybrid chatbots based on intent recognition.Starting from a case study in the field of food delivery, the paper examines how these technologies influence operational efficiency, user experience and business process control.The conclusions show that, for small and medium-sized enterprises, rule-based chatbots often represent the most cost-effective solution, while hybrid chatbots become economically justified in contexts characterized by high volume and high diversity of interactions.
