## Resumo disponível

Social isolation is an important factor in accelerating cognitive decline for patients with Alzheimer’s Disease, yet conventional digital interfaces remain inaccessible due to motor and sensory barriers. This paper presents ALMA, an empathetic multimodal companion designed to mediate the relationship between patients and caregivers through a natural Voice User Interface. The system integrates a dual architecture: a physical Totem interface for the patient, which leverages Generative AI for digital reminiscence therapy and emotional support, and a dedicated family application for managing digital memories. Our implementation utilizes Large Language Models optimized for low-latency interaction, facilitating fluid conversation. Preliminary results indicate that removing tactile interaction significantly reduces the user’s cognitive load, facilitating consistent mental stimulation and mitigating the feeling of loneliness.
