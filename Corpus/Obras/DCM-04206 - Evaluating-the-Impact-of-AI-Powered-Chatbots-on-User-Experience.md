## Resumo disponível

This study explores the impact of AI-driven chatbots on user experience in digital environments, examining how chatbots enhance customer engagement, satisfaction, and operational efficiency. The research highlights both the advantages and challenges associated with chatbot implementation, providing key recommendations for optimizing chatbot design to improve user interactions.
