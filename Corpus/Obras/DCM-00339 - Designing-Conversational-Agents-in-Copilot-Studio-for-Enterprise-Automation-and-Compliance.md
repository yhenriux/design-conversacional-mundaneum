## Resumo disponível

The presented paper examines how conversational agents can be implemented and designed in the Microsoft Copilot Studio with a special consideration given to how they may be used to automate enterprise operations as well as comply with regulatory issues. These agents enhance the productivity, teamwork and confidence in human-AI settings in the integration of intelligent task decomposition, contextual awareness and user-role adaptivity. Research data demonstrates that Copilot Studio Agents are more accurate, efficient and user friendly, as compared to static flows. Other issues pinpointed in the paper include overdependence, ethical issues and openness. Our simulations, tests of performance evaluation, and methods of trust calibration allow us to build a complete picture of creating solid and enterprise-level conversational agents according to the perspective of compliance and automation.
