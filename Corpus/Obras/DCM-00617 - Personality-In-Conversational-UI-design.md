## Resumo disponível

<p>This paper describes mathematical representations for personality as an emergence, in the designof conversational UI, from voice, video and image. We use SoulMachines conversational UI as a casestudy, for the determination of data structures for personality definition. This model differs fromother models in the existence of several aspects of personalities, with the possibility of the stabilityof one or more atomic aspects of a personality. Thus naturalness exists for the definition of apersonality</p>
