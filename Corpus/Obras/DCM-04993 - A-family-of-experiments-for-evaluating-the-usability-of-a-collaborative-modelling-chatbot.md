## Resumo disponível

Recent natural language processing developments have facilitated the adoption of chatbots in typically collaborative software engineering tasks. Families of experiments can overcome limitations in terms of the sample size of individual experiments. To experimentally evaluate the usability of a chatbot for collaborative modelling (i.e., SOCIO) and tackle some of the typical shortcomings of individual experiments, we conducted a family of three experiments to evaluate the usability of SOCIO against the Creately online collaborative tool.
