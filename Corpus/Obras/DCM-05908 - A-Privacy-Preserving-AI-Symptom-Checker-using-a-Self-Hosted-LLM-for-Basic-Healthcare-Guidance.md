## Resumo disponível

Patients often need quick answers to health questions, but hospital systems usually don't offer instant help. While commercial AI chatbots seem like a solution, they create huge privacy risks. Patients may share extremely sensitive information, sometimes in emotional moments, about their personal health, relationships, or even mention their family members' names. Sending this private data to third-party APIs can lead to dangerous data leaks. This paper presents a framework to solve this critical problem. We propose a secure, self-hosted AI chatbot that is fully integrated within a hospital's own private system. This ensures that sensitive patient conversations are never exposed to outside companies. The system uses a local Large Language Model (LLM) like Llama 3, which runs entirely on the hospital's own server. For registered patients, a secure verification system allows the chatbot to access their health records safely, providing even more accurate and personalized answers without compromising their privacy. This framework not only provides a secure and cost-effective alternative to commercial AI but also establishes a new standard for patient-centric care where trust, privacy, and immediate support are paramount.
