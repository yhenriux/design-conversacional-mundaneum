## Resumo disponível

This exploratory research analyzes the impacts of chatbots that were created for the purpose of supporting and informing financial decisions.Previous research discusses the impacts of chatbots that were created to support and inform financial decisions in the financial service industry.This research takes a different approach by also analyzing the impacts of chatbots that were created to support and inform financial decisions both inside and outside higher education too.To assess the impact of the use of chatbots created for the purpose of supporting and informing financial decisions, I analyzed secondary data from thirty three sources.The analyzed sources suggested that chatbots were able to productively support and inform financial decisions.The sources also suggested that while chatbot technology is increasing its capability to better communicate with humans, the use of human interaction and assistance are still valuable.These findings suggest that while chatbots will continue to productively support and inform financial decisions, they will not replace the value human interaction can provide.
