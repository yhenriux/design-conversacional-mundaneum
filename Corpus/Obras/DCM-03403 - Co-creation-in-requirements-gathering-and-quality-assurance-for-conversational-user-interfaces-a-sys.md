## Resumo disponível

BACKGROUND Conversational User Interfaces (CUIs) have brought many benefits across a variety of sectors but also present many complexities. Integrating co-creation into CUI requirements gathering and quality assurance is essential to advancing the design of humanity centric CUIs, but this brings many challenges. OBJECTIVE This paper provides a systematic review of the literature outlining how human stakeholder participants and AI tools are engaged in the co-creation of CUIs, with a specific emphasis on the requirements elicitation and quality assurance processes. METHODS Seven bibliographic databases were considered, but only Web of Science and ScienceDirect were included. This was based on specificity and consideration of the gold-standard reference set. Search terms covered co-creation and CUIs, also known as chatbots. Thirty-three studies were included in the final review. RESULTS Results showed that the majority of studies involved both end-users and domain experts (63.6%) and primarily used purposive or convenience sampling, with all adopting multiple methods - most commonly interviews, prototyping, and workshops - to gather requirements. Stakeholder feedback was typically integrated before, during, and after prototyping, reflecting an iterative co-creation approach with limited use of AI-assisted methods. Stakeholder feedback shaped user interface design, features, conversation flow, and content, with safety and ethics addressed in only a third of the studies. Methods varied across domains, with interviewing and prototyping being the most common. Co-creation was mostly termed co-design. Use of theoretical frameworks was inconsistent, but human-centred or user-centred design were the most common approaches. CONCLUSIONS Results are presented in terms of how co-creation is integrated into requirements gathering and quality assurance, compared with best practice guidelines, and are used to discuss how a toolkit might best support developers in fully integrating co-creation into the requirements gathering and quality assurance processes when designing chatbots.
