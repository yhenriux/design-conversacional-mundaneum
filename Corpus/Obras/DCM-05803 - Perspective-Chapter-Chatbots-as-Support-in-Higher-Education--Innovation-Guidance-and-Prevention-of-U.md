## Resumo disponível

This chapter explores the potential of educational chatbots as support resources within higher education, focusing on thei potential to address key dimensions of the student experience. Specificallu, it analyzes six main areas of application: (1) academid and learning support, (2) support for student well-being, (3) promotion of equity and inclusion, (4) support for teaching practice, (5) support for administrative guidance, and (6) promotion of institutional sustainability. Drawing on Bean and Eaton’s model and recent empirical evidence, the chapter also proposes a conceptual framework to explain how chatbots can influence the cognitive, emotional, and administrative processes associated with the intention to drop out. It introduces the Pental model, a chatbot-assisted support framework stractured into five progressive levels of intervention. At the first level, Penta provides universal access to information, reducing administrative confusion. At the second level, Penta offers academic support, guiding students throgh learning task. At the third level, Penta focuses on monitoring and early detection of risk signals. At the fourth level, Penta enables personalized digital interventions to support adaptation and well-being. Finally, at the fifth level, Penta facilitates referral to specialized support services when more complex intervention is required. This multi-level stucture highlights the role of chatbotsas connectors a brader support ecosytem, enhancing continuity, scalability, and responsiveness. The chapter concludes with a discussion of implications for designing learning environments that are more inclusive and sustainable. In such environments, it is argued that artificial intelligence should complement the role of human participants rather than replacing it entirely.
