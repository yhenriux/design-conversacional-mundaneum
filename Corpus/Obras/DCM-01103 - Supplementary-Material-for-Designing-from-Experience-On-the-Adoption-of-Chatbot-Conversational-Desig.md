## Resumo disponível

Supplementary material for the paper entitled Designing from Experience: On the Adoption of Chatbot Conversational Design Practices by Practitioners. This repository contains the following files: conversation_samples -> Conversations made by the participants in the case study stages. In each file, the conversation from the left was made without the guide, and the one from the right was created with the guide; guide -> Faithful prints of the web pages of the guide that was shared with the participants for them to read before developing the second conversation sample; interview_transcripts -> Transcripts of the interviews conducted with each participant at the last stage of the case study; instructions_to_participants.pdf -> File provided to participants containing the instructions for each step of the case study; narratives.pdf -> Narratives generated after the analysis of transcripts and conversations. transcripts_coding.xlsx -> Sheet containing transcripts from participants' responses and the corresponding code after the thematic analysis;
