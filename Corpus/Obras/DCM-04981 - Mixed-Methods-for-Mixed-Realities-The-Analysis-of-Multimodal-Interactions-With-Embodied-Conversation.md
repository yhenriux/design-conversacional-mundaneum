## Resumo disponível

Multimodal interactions with anthropomorphic virtual agents are increasingly becoming the subject of current research on human-agent interaction, but existing research paradigms often focus only on the user's perception of interactions and do not position the emergent interaction processes themselves as the research object.The methodological approach presented here addresses this problem and focuses on both the relationship level as well as the content level in human-agent interaction.The combination of mixed reality representations and mixed methods allows, among other things, for the identification of possible discrepancies between the user's individual experiences and their physically expressed behavior during these interactions.
