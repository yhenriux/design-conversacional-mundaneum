## Resumo disponível

The CareCompanion mobile application provides a platform for caregivers of Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) to connect, share experiences, and support one another. It integrates several features, including a forum for discussions, a chat and connection system for building relationships, an events section for discovering relevant programs, and wellness tools such as guided breathing exercises, customisable soundscapes, and daily inspirational quotes. A key highlight is the AI companion chatbot, designed with four distinct personalities to offer emotional reassurance, practical guidance, humor, and assistance. To ensure relevant and adaptive interactions, a custom context-awareness method is developed. Built using Flutter and Firebase, CareCompanion supports both Android and iOS. Thematic frequency analysis of user feedback and survey results from usability tests show positive engagement of participants. The project successfully affirms the emotional support CareCompanion renders, with ongoing improvements planned for future deployment.
