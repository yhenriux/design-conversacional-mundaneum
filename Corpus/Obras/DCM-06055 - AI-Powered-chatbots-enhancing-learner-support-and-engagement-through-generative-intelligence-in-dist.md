## Resumo disponível

The digital transformation of distance education has historically struggled with the "transactional distance", a psychological and communicative gap between instructor and learner. This manuscript investigates the transformative role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) powered chatbots in mitigating this gap. By synthesizing contemporary research from 2023–2026, the study examines the evolution of conversational agents from rule-based scripts to Large Language Models (LLMs) and Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. The manuscript analyzes the impact of chatbots through three psychological lenses: cognitive, affective, and social, while detailing the technical integration via the Chatbot LMS Integration Framework (CLIF). Global institutional case studies provide empirical evidence of enhanced enrollment, improved retention, and 24x7 personalized mentoring. However, critical challenges regarding the "loneliness epidemic," factual "hallucinations," cognitive dependence, and data privacy are addressed. The study concludes with a "hybrid pedagogy" roadmap for 2026 and beyond, recommending Socratic tutoring models to ensure that AI complements rather than replaces human-centered instruction.
