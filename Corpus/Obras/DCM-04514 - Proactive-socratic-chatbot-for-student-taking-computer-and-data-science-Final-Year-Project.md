## Resumo disponível

Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED) has been significantly advanced by Large Language Models (LLMs). The LLMs enable interactive and personalised learning experiences. However, the current LLM-based chatbots are primarily designed to provide direct answers, which introduce a key pedagogical concern. This answer delivery paradigm may reduce students’ engagement in reasoning processes and lead to metacognitive laziness. To address this limitation, this work proposes Persocratic Tutor, a multi-agent Socratic chatbot designed to shift the interaction paradigm from answer delivery to guided reasoning. The system uses a hybrid LLM and Intelligent Tutoring System (ITS) framework, consisting of four agents: Question Understanding, Student Modelling, Strategy Formulation and Question Generation. This enables adaptive instructional guidance in accordance with the student’s mastery level. Additionally, the system uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ensure grounded responses in reliable domain knowledge. The system is evaluated through extensive testing, which includes stability testing, coverage testing, multi-turn continuity analysis, and user study involving 30 participants. Results show that the system consistently produces stable categorical outputs and maintains a 100% question-driven interaction ratio, demonstrating a strong adherence to Socratic dialogue principles. Coverage testing further confirms that the system is able to dynamically apply appropriate pedagogical strategies based on mastery levels. Finally, the user evaluation indicated that 87%, 63%, and 67% of participants reported knowledge improvement in concept, procedure, and next-step tasks respectively, with overall positive feedback on guided learning experience. Overall, these findings demonstrate that Persocratic Tutor provides a practical and effective framework for Socratic and adaptive learning support. By integrating guided questioning within an LLM-based system, this work highlights a promising direction for developing human-centric and cognitively engaging educational AI systems.
