## Resumo disponível

Digitalisation has changed how Finnish banks deliver customer service, and many banks now use AI chatbots alongside human staff. Chatbots can respond quickly and are available around the clock, but it remains uncertain whether they meet customer expectations in situations that require trust or emotional support. This study compares how customers rate human service and AI chatbots in Finnish banks and what affects their satisfaction, trust, and preferences. The study used a quantitative online survey and drew on the SERVQUAL model and the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM). The results showed that human representatives scored higher ratings in reliability, clarity, and trust, and were clearly preferred for complex or sensitive issues. Chatbots worked well for simple tasks but had difficulties with detailed questions and language limitations. The findings suggest that chatbots work better as a support tool than as a full replacement. A hybrid model, where chatbots deal with routine tasks, and humans handle more complex cases, seems to match customer expectations and helps create more balanced, customer centred banking services.
