## Resumo disponível

Our study developed a lightweight AI chatbot system for e-commerce customer service in the Smartshop platform. Our study aimed at exploring whether a small-scale large language model can provide e-commerce customer service support that is efficient, reliable, and maintainable for small and medium-sized e-commerce enterprises with limited hardware resources. Our research used Hybrid methods, mixing literature review, design science research and empirical testing. Based on the findings of previous research, a modular chatbot system was designed and implemented using the Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct model, 8-bit quantization, and a decoupled knowledge base. This system was tested by using 10 classical e-commerce customer service scenarios in a Midto-low-end hardware platforms. The findings show that the proposed chatbot system can achieve the pre-defined functional and nonfunctional requirement. During the test, it achieved intent identified accuracy and consistency of facts by 100%. In addition, 8-bit quantization limits static VRAM consumption reduced from 1180MB to 680 MB, reducing VRAM consumption by 42.37%. The average response time was 1.77 second, lower than a pre-defined 5-second threshold. The findings of research show that a small-scale large language model can provide a practical and effective solution for e-commerce customer service. Modular design and decoupled knowledge base can enhance the maintainability and scalability of the system. The main limitation of the study was the small number of tested circumstances, and lack of high-concurrency evaluation.
