## Resumo disponível

In today's world, as we are moving toward the modernization of technology. The company is more and more involved in leveling up its technology by introducing AI. And as per this context from a certain period, the introduction of chatbots has increased as a value-creating tool in human life and has proved vital. Chatbots are computer system that uses AI and takes user input and answers it via text, audio, or both without any person's intervention between the chatbot and the primary user. Chatbots provide a sense that a user is not talking to a computer programming it seems like having a conversation with a human being. The purpose of this research is to gather information that how youth think about the increased introduction and usage of chatbots in the market and do they are interested in the adoption of chatbots in their life for creating value. The research was brought off through the determination and application of the quantitative methods by collecting metrics from various sources that are available throughout the web and analyzing it descriptively and presenting it via graphs and qualitative The target population covered for the research is the student residing in Greater Noida. The chatbot can play a crucial role to reduce stress by providing certain therapy according to the person's needs.
