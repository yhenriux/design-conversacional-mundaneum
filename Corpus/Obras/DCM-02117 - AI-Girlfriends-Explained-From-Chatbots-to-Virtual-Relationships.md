## Resumo disponível

This document explores the evolution of AI girlfriends, tracing the shift from early chatbot systems to emotionally responsive virtual relationships. It examines how advances in artificial intelligence, memory modeling, and conversational design have enabled deeper, more continuous digital companionship experiences. The paper discusses key trends shaping AI relationships, including emotional continuity, personalization, and long-term interaction, as well as the social and technological factors driving adoption across different user groups. The goal is to provide a clear, high-level overview of how AI girlfriends are redefining human–AI interaction in modern digital environments.
