## Resumo disponível

This collection of research papers presents the development of various AI-driven chatbot systems designed to enhance user interaction and decision-making across diverse fields such as healthcare, tourism, fashion, and equipment maintenance.A primary focus is on integrating advanced AI techniques, including deep learning for image classification, natural language processing (NLP), and large language models (LLMs), to create sophisticated, multimodal applications.Key projects include a virtual health assistant leveraging a fine-tuned LLaMA3 model and SigLIP Vision Text Transformer to provide preliminary diagnoses from both text and medical images, achieving 88% accuracy [1-3]; a healthcare chatbot for hearing-impaired individuals that recognizes Arabic Sign Language (ArSL) using a fine-tuned VGG16 model with 99.14% accuracy and generates responses via Google's Gemini LLM [4,5]; and a comparative evaluation of AI chatbots like GPT-4V, Claude-3, and Gemini for curating gene interactions from biological pathway images, where GPT-4V demonstrated superior performance [6,7].Other applications feature a fashion chatbot that uses body part and item segmentation (CDCL, Mask R-CNN) to find similar clothing styles and recommend coordinators [8-10]; a system for recognizing Makkah landmarks using InceptionV3 and MobileNetV2 to provide historical information to tourists via image recognition and a retrieval-based chatbot [11, 12]; and a centralized equipment repair notification system that uses a LINE chatbot integrated with Cloud Vision AI and Gemini to process text and image-based repair requests with high accuracy and user satisfaction [13][14][15][16].Collectively, these studies underscore the potential of AI-powered chatbots to bridge communication gaps, streamline complex processes, and improve access to information through intelligent, user-friendly interfaces.
