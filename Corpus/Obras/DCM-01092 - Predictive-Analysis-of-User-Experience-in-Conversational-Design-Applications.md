## Resumo disponível

This study examines the predictive relationships between six conversational design capability dimensions, namely dialogue flow quality, conversational personalisation, response accuracy, multimodal interaction, error recovery grace, and contextual awareness, and the User Experience Index across N = 524 active users of conversational applications spanning five application categories. CFA confirms a six-factor measurement model with excellent fit (CFI = .962, RMSEA = .041). SEM establishes dialogue flow quality (beta = .46, p < .001) and conversational personalisation (beta = .43, p < .001) as dominant user experience predictors, with digital literacy level significantly moderating all conversational design to user experience pathways. XGBoost achieves R squared = .839, identifying natural dialogue progression, session-persistent memory, and factually accurate contextual responses as the three highest-impact item-level SHAP predictors. Findings provide actionable guidance for conversational UX designers, chatbot platform architects, and digital product strategists.
