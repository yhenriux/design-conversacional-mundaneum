## Resumo disponível

Kolkata currently does have the status of a 'smart city' in South Asia, really on account of the proactive approach of the government towards modern technology, particularly the one encompassing artificial intelligence. This research analysis focuses predominantly on artificial intelligence implementations such as chatbots in the transformation of service delivery within the city of Kolkata. The specific aims of this study were to ascertain the usage extent of chatbots in various government departments, their role in facilitating public administration services, and distantly conducted administrative communications in an efficient manner. The study, in particular, addresses the opportunities and threats associated with chatbot integration such as comprehension and composition of scenario-based questions within the micro text-level scope, that is, solutions unfeasible to resolve immediately via internet searches, e.g., awkward scenario intervention through AI on a quite small scale; designs where the user is required to issue immediate reactive stimuli; and situations where employees can directly supervise the intervention domain, as well as inherent data risks within an organization. The current study tries to take into consideration all global and national concerns with respect to each of the case studies with focus on Kolkata. Accordingly, let us propose solutions of which an important chunk will be AI-compatible training, IoT incorporation, and updating of data replenishment standards. All of this would be needed to address the issues and to facilitate a speedy growth of digital democracy-enabled programs within the city
