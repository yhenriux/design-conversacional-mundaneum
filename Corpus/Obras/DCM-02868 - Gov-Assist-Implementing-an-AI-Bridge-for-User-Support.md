## Resumo disponível

GovAssist is an AI-powered chatbot designed to streamline public interactions with government services, enhancing user experience across various departments. This intelligent virtual assistant acts as a$24 / 7$helpdesk, providing real-time support, answering common queries, and guiding users through government procedures and services. Leveraging natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning, GovAssist understands user inquiries and delivers accurate, timely responses while continually learning and improving through interactions. The chatbot offers a user-friendly interface that simplifies access to information such as service application processes, document requirements, grievance filing, and more. By automating routine tasks and offering immediate assistance, GovAssist reduces the need for human intervention and enhances user satisfaction with government services. Its flexible and modular design ensures smooth integration with existing government websites and portals, making it a scalable solution for diverse administrative needs. With security and data privacy as core principles, GovAssist adheres to strict government regulations, ensuring that all user interactions and information exchanges remain secure. This project aims to revolutionize the way citizens interact with government services, fostering a more transparent, efficient, and accessible public service environment through the power of artificial intelligence.
