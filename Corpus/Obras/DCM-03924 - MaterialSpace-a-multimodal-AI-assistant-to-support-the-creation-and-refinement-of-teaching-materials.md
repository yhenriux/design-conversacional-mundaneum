## Resumo disponível

As the adoption of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) expands in educational settings,teachers increasingly face the challenge of creating multimodal teaching materials for digitalclassrooms. Although current Conversational User Interfaces (CUIs) facilitate initial ideation,they cause a high cognitive load due to the semantic gap associated with the complexity ofprompt engineering. Consequently, teachers struggle to create to create structured, pedagogi-cally aligned multimedia artifacts with GenAI.To address this research gap, this research investigates how a multimodal, worksheet-centeredinterface can mitigate these barriers for lower secondary school teachers. Through a four-phaseUser-Centered Design (UCD) process, the GenAI worksheet prototype MaterialSpace was de-veloped. To overcome the limitations of unstructured conversational prompting, the systemutilizes a persistent worksheet canvas, multimodal interactions, and conversational clarificationquestions to scaffold the user’s intent.The final evaluation demonstrated that embedding explicit pedagogical parameters directly intothe configuration significantly reduces the friction of intention expression. Specifically, 87.5%of the participants confirmed that the prototype met their worksheet creation requirements and75% expressed a strong intention for future use. These findings demonstrate that guided clarifi-cation within an artifact-centered workspace successfully reduces the cognitive load of promptengineering, establishing AI as a transparent and reliable pedagogical partner, which leads tohigh levels of usability, trust, and technology acceptance.
