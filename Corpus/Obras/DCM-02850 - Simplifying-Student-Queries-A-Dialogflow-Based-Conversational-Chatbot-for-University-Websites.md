## Resumo disponível

In the rapidly evolving academic field, universities are increasingly challenged to enhance student engagement and streamline access to essential information. This paper presents the project, an innovative conversational chatbot designed to provide 24/7 support for students, faculty, and staff within the university community. The primary motivation behind this project is to address the inefficiencies of traditional communication methods, offering a user-friendly platform that facilitates immediate access to information and assistance. The project outlines specific objectives, including improving administrative efficiency, addressing student needs, and enhancing user experience across digital platforms. Utilizing Dialogflow, a powerful natural language understanding platform developed by Google, the chatbot is trained to understand and respond to user queries effectively through intents, entities, and contexts. The expected outcomes of this project include increased user satisfaction, improved engagement with university services, and reduced administrative burdens. It aims to change the way information is accessible and support is given in higher education institutions by promoting a more connected academic environment.
