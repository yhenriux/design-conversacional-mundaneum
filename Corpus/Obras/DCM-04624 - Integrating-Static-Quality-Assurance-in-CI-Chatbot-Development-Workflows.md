## Resumo disponível

Chatbots are an emerging, disruptive technology to access software services via natural language conversation. Companies are adopting task-oriented chatbots to offer customer support, widen availability, lower the entry barrier to their services (e.g., for shopping, booking, or banking), and keep users engaged. This interest in chatbots has promoted the appearance of many chatbot development platforms and tools, like Google’s Dialogflow, Amazon’s Lex, or Rasa. However, there are hardly any proposals to integrate automated quality assurance mechanisms – including metrics and static analysis – into the chatbot development workflow. To fill this gap, we present a continuous integration workflow for chatbot development, implemented as GitHub actions, and show its usefulness by its application to open-source chatbots.
