ABSTRACT E-Learning has become more and more popular in recent years with the advance of new technologies. Using their mobile devices, people can expand their knowledge anytime and anywhere. E-Learning also makes it possible for people to manage their learning progression freely and follow their own learning style. However, studies show that E-Learning can cause the user to experience feelings of isolation and detachment due to the lack of human-like interactions in most E-Learning platforms. These feelings could reduce the user’s motivation to learn. In this paper, we explore and evaluate how well current chatbot technologies assist users’ learning on E-Learning platforms and how these technologies could possibly reduce problems such as feelings of isolation and detachment.
