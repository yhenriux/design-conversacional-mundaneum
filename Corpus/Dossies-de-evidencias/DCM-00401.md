However, the mechanisms by which chatbot design features influence UX remain understudied, particularly regarding the roles of emotional and cognitive mediators. Methods: This study employed a 2 × 2 within-subjects experimental design with 160 participants to investigate the effects of anthropomorphism (high vs. low) and perceived intelligence (high vs. low) in chatbot avatars on UX. Structural equation modeling (SEM) was utilized to analyze the mediating roles of perceived empathy and trust in this relationship. Results: Direct effects of anthropomorphism and perceived intelligence on UX were nonsignificant. However, their combined influence was significantly mediated by perceived empathy and trust ( β = 0.48, * p* < 0.01). Specifically, highly anthropomorphic avatars correlated with elevated empathy ( β = 0.32) and trust (β = 0.27), which in turn improved UX.
