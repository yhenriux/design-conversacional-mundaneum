The earliest chatbot, ELIZA, created in the 1960s, used pattern matching and scripted responses to simulate a conversatio n with a psychotherapist[15]. However, these early systems were still heavily rule-based, and their ability to engage in meaningful conversations was limited by rigid programming[19].
