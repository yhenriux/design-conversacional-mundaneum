System Sgt Blackwell (figure 1) was originally constructed as a technological showpiece at an information kiosk for the 2004 Army Science Conference. Since then, Blackwell and other characters based on his design have been developed for a variety of entertainment, educational and training applications (e,g.,. Traum et al, 2007). ASR input is sent to a classifier based on cross-language information retrieval techniques, which was trained on a set of utterance-answer mappings.
