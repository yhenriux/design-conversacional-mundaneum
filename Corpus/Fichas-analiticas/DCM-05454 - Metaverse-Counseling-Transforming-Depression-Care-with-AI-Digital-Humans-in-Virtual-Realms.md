## Resumo disponível

Abstract In this paper, we present an innovative solution to address the problem of modern depression through a metaverse-based counseling platform. Recognizing that time, space, and social factors make it difficult for individuals to access counseling, the platform leverages NPCs in the metaverse. The platform analyzes user text data to predict suicide risk and emotional state using models built on datasets such as ‘Suicide and Depression Detection’ and various algorithms such as K-nearest neighbors, logistic regression, and SVM. To implement digital human conversations in Unreal Engine, we used several plugins, including speech-to-text, chatbot, and text-to-speech, to process user speech in real time to enable effective counseling responses. Ultimately, the platform aims to provide accessible counseling services and personalized support to depression patients by overcoming temporal and spatial constraints. This metaverse solution is expected to not only break new ground in the treatment of depression, but also contribute significantly to addressing mental health issues, enabling an innovative approach to modern well-being.
