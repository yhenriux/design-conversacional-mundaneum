## Resumo disponível

A chatbot is a software that is used to interact between a computer and a human in natural language.The chatbot application provided in this paper helps the users to access the college related information along with the functionality of college map from any location with internet connection.This system reduces the work of college administration and the staff in case of answering all the basic queries of the students.The proposed system makes use of AIML along with NLP in order to provide responses to the user.It has made the use of client-server communication for providing faster responses to the users.
