## Resumo disponível

The rapid proliferation of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies in human resource management has fundamentally transformed the landscape of talent acquisition and recruitment across modern organizations. This study empirically investigates the impact of AI-driven tools and processes on Talent Acquisition Effectiveness and Recruitment Efficiency in organizations located in Chennai. Seven independent variables—AI-Based Resume Screening Efficiency, Use of Machine Learning Algorithms in Candidate Selection, AI-Driven Candidate Matching Accuracy, Automation of Recruitment Processes, AI Chatbots for Candidate Communication and Engagement, Predictive Analytics in Talent Acquisition, and Integration of AI Tools in HR Recruitment Systems—were examined in relation to two dependent variables: Talent Acquisition Effectiveness and Recruitment Efficiency in Modern Organizations. A descriptive and causal research design was adopted, and primary data were collected from 292 respondents comprising HR Managers, Talent Acquisition Specialists, Recruitment Officers, HR Consultants, and organizational employees engaged in hiring processes across Chennai using a structured questionnaire based on a five-point Likert scale. Purposive sampling was employed as the primary sampling technique. Statistical tools including descriptive analysis, Pearson correlation, and multiple regression analysis were applied using IBM SPSS Statistics 26. The findings reveal that all seven AI-related variables positively and significantly influence both dependent variables. AIDriven Candidate Matching Accuracy emerged as the strongest predictor (β = 0.336), followed by Integration of AI Tools in HR Recruitment Systems and Predictive Analytics in Talent Acquisition. The regression model explains 89.9% of variance in recruitment outcomes, indicating substantial explanatory power. The study provides actionable implications for HR practitioners, organizational leaders, and technology developers committed to leveraging AI for strategic talent management.
