## Resumo disponível

This bachelor's thesis investigated, as a narrative literature review, how the design of chatbots affects customer experience. The aim of the work was to create a comprehensive overview of the key features of the design, the psychological mechanisms behind them, and their effects on the customer experience. The results of the review shows that successful customer experience depends above all on the conversational quality and technical reliability of the chatbot. The key conclusion is that effective chatbot design is always context-specific: there is no one-size-fitsall solution, but the strategy must be tailored to the customer and the situation.
