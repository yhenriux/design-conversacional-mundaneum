## Resumo disponível

Production lines are widely used in large-scale manufacturing, where workload balancing across stations is crucial. In practice, unexpected events such as equipment failures often disrupt this balance, requiring rapid reallocation of tasks. While optimization methods can generate rebalancing solutions, operators may struggle to interpret and apply them under time constraints. Task-oriented conversational agents offer a promising way to bridge this gap by enabling natural language interaction. This paper compares two dialogue system architectures for guiding operators in rebalancing tasks: (i) an end-to-end neural model and (ii) a modular LLM-powered pipeline. Two synthetic datasets were generated for evaluation. Results show that the modular pipeline achieves higher extraction accuracy and lower latency than the end-to-end model. These results highlight the benefits of modularity for deploying conversational assistants in industrial production environments.
