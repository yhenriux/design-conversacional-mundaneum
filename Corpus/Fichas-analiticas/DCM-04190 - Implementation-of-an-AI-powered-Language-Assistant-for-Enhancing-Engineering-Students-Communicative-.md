## Resumo disponível

This study investigates the impact of an AI-powered language assistant on improving the communicative proficiency of engineering students through mobile technologies.Recognising the critical role of communicative skills in engineering education, this research addresses the gap between technical expertise and effective communication.By integrating an AI-driven chatbot designed for language learning into the curriculum, the study explores its efficacy in enhancing students' abilities in technical writing, oral communication, and interdisciplinary collaboration.A quasi-experimental design involving 100 undergraduate engineering students from the Institute of Energy at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University was employed, with data collected through pre-and post-intervention assessments, surveys, and interviews.The findings demonstrate significant improvements in the experimental group's language proficiency, particularly in speaking and listening skills.High levels of student engagement and positive feedback underscore the potential of AI-powered tools to transform traditional language education, making it more interactive and accessible.The study highlights the importance of integrating advanced technological solutions in educational practices to prepare engineering students for the global professional environment.
