## Resumo disponível

Despite the widespread integration of artificial intelligence (AI) chatbots into e-commerce platforms, their effectiveness in enhancing customer satisfaction remains uncertain, particularly in the Malaysian context. Many consumers report issues related to usability, responsiveness, trust, empathy, and accessibility, raising questions about the true value these tools provide. This study aims to examine the effects of key AI chatbot attributes, namely usability, responsiveness, perceived trust, empathy, and accessibility, on customer satisfaction in the e-commerce sector. A quantitative research design was employed, utilizing a structured online questionnaire administered to a sample of 384 Shopee users in Selangor, selected through simple random sampling. The instrument, consisting of validated measurement items, underwent expert review and pilot testing to ensure content validity and reliability. Data were analyzed using the Statistical Package for the Social Sciences (SPSS), and regression analysis was conducted to assess the effects of the independent variables on customer satisfaction. The results indicate that all five chatbot attributes have statistically significant positive effects on customer satisfaction. Among these, usability demonstrated the strongest influence (β = 0.431, p < 0.001), followed by accessibility, responsiveness, empathy, and perceived trust. These findings highlight the critical role of intuitive interface design and functional accessibility in enhancing user satisfaction with AI chatbot services. The study offers practical insights for improving chatbot design to better meet user expectations and enhance satisfaction. It also informs policymakers aiming to foster inclusive and user-focused digital services in Malaysia's e-commerce sector.
